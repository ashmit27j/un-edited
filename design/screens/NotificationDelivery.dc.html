Shown once, on the second day, above the Home edition strip. Never during onboarding.

Rules

  1. The edition is one a day, on the days the reader picks, and never carries a headline. Big story, topic and source alerts share one cap the reader sets (1, 3 or 5 a day; 3 by default). Downloads finished doesn’t count.
  2. Big story = covered by at least 5 of the reader’s sources (or 60%, whichever is lower) within 6 hours. One per story, ever.
  3. Quiet hours (10 PM to 7 AM by default) hold everything until they end. There are no urgent alerts.
  4. Tapping opens: Morning Edition → Home; Big story → the Article with all versions; Download → the folder.
  5. Language follows the app language, headlines stay in the publisher’s language.
  6. Guests can turn on Morning Edition only; it is stored on the device.
