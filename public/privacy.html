Un:edited

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Un:edited is a free news reader. It shows stories as publishers wrote them, from the outlets you choose. This page explains what information the app handles and why. It covers the Android app and the website at unedited-bulletin.vercel.app.

What we collect

  • Account details, if you sign in. Your email address, or the name and email on the Google account you sign in with. Signing in is optional; you can read as a guest.
  • Your reading choices. The outlets you follow, saved stories, reading history, language and display settings. On a guest device these stay on the device. If you sign in, they are also stored with your account so they follow you across devices.
  • Notification details, if you turn notifications on. A push token for your device, and the times you pick for your daily edition.
  • Problem reports, if you send one. When you use "Report a problem" on a story, we store the story, the problem you picked and any details you write, so we can fix it.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider (Vercel) and backend provider (Supabase) may log IP address, browser or device type, and request times to keep the service running and secure.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or sell your information.
  • We don't use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't write or alter the stories you read. Articles come from the publishers' own feeds and pages and are shown as published.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your reading choices in sync.
  • To deliver the notifications you asked for.
  • To run, secure and fix the service.

Who processes it

We use these providers to run Un:edited. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts, synced settings and the news backend.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Google: only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android), Apple and browser push services (website): only if you turn on notifications.

When you open a story on a publisher's site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and the data stored with it, email us at the address below and we'll remove it. Guest data lives only on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Children

Un:edited is a general news reader and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
